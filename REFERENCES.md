# References

These are the papers we worked from. The PDFs are not in this repository, because they belong to their publishers. Links go to the publisher of record.

## Control of robotic ankles

R. Jiménez-Fabián and O. Verlinden, "Review of control algorithms for robotic ankle systems in lower-limb orthoses, prostheses, and exoskeletons," *Medical Engineering & Physics*, vol. 34, no. 4, pp. 397-408, 2012. [doi:10.1016/j.medengphy.2011.11.018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.medengphy.2011.11.018)

This was our main control reference. It covers gait phase identification, adapting to different walking conditions, and recognising motion intention.

## Devices we studied

The graduation book works through four active ankle designs in detail. Our mechanical concept ended up closest to AMP 2.0.

**AMP 2.0**, the Ankle Mimicking Prosthetic foot, built around a four-bar mechanism with a stepped actuation sequence.

**SPARKy 3**, the Spring Ankle with Regenerative Kinetics, which uses robotic tendon actuation.

**PKU-RoboTPro-II**, which combines a magnetic encoder, a Maxon RE-40 150 W actuator, a six-axis force/torque sensor and an STM32F103 controller.

**A bionic running prosthesis for unilateral transtibial military amputees**, a dual-motor design with an extra degree of freedom.
