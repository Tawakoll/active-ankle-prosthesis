## Control of robotic ankles

- R. Jiménez-Fabián and O. Verlinden, "Review of control algorithms for robotic ankle systems in lower-limb orthoses, prostheses, and exoskeletons," *Medical Engineering & Physics*, vol. 34, no. 4, pp. 397–408, 2012. [doi:10.1016/j.medengphy.2011.11.018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.medengphy.2011.11.018)

  The primary control reference for this project — gait phase identification, adaptation to walking conditions, and motion intention recognition.

## Devices studied

The graduation book surveys four active ankle designs in detail; the mechanical concept here is closest to **AMP 2.0**.

- **AMP 2.0** — Ankle Mimicking Prosthetic foot 2.0. Four-bar mechanism, stepped actuation sequence.
- **SPARKy 3** — Spring Ankle with Regenerative Kinetics, robotic tendon actuation.
- **PKU-RoboTPro-II** — magnetic encoder, Maxon RE-40 150 W actuator, six-axis force/torque sensor, STM32F103 control.
- **Bionic running prosthesis for unilateral transtibial military amputees** — dual-motor design with an additional degree of freedom.
